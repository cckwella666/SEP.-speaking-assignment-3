More and more cafes, and even some offices, are starting to allow pets inside. For a lot of people, this is wonderful news. Having a dog nearby can lower stress and make the day feel less lonely. Pet owners often say their animals are calm and well-trained, so there's no real problem. But not everyone agrees. Some people are allergic to animals, and some just don't feel comfortable around dogs or cats. There are also questions about hygiene, especially in places where food is served. So here's the question: should more places open their doors to pets, or is it better to keep some spaces animal-free?

⚖️ Debate Challenge — Cold Start

Motion: "Pets should be allowed in more public places, like cafes and offices."

Your position: choose in class  |  Prep time: 0 minutes (cold)

You'll receive this motion with no advance notice in class. You'll have 90 seconds to decide your position, then go straight into a 3-round live rebuttal exchange with your teacher.

Key vocabulary (study these before class — you won't have prep time for content)

allowed
被允許的
Dogs are allowed in this cafe.
comfort
安慰；舒適
Pets can bring comfort to lonely people.
hygiene
衛生
Hygiene is important in restaurants.
distraction
分心的事物
A noisy pet can be a distraction at work.
companion
陪伴者
A dog is a loyal companion.
policy
政策
The office changed its pet policy this year.
🎯 Goal this week: it's okay not to have a perfect answer ready. Practice staying in the exchange — try phrases like "That's a fair point, but…" to buy yourself a second to think, instead of going silent.

⚡ Spontaneous Speaking (no prep!)

Pick one card. Speak for 60–90 seconds. No planning time — just talk.

Describe a pet you've had or wanted.
Talk about a time an animal made you feel better.
Describe a public place where you saw an animal.
Talk about whether you'd bring a pet to work.
